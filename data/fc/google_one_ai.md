What is the difference between AI Premium and a Google AI Plus plan?

The Google AI Premium plan has a new name: Google AI Plus. It includes the same benefits like the Gemini app, Gemini Notebook, 400 GB of storage, and more.

What’s the difference between YouTube Premium individual and YouTube Premium Lite plans?

The YouTube Premium individual plan unlocks all of YouTube and YouTube Music ad-free, offline, and in the background. [12](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/#footnote:ytp) Enjoy every Premium feature across all content types.

The YouTube Premium Lite plan is a good fit for you if you mainly watch creator content. [8](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/#footnote:ytp_lite) Music videos, concerts, and songs may still have ads and won't be available offline or in the background.

What’s the difference between Fitbit Premium and Google Health Premium?

Google Health Premium replaces the former Fitbit Premium subscription with a singular place to manage and control your health data. The Google Health app simplifies and brings all of your data into one place – including your Fitbit, Google Fit, Health Connect and Apple Health data plus your personal medical records. It also introduces Google Health Coach, which is built with Gemini to be a fitness trainer, sleep coach, and health and wellness advisor, available across the Fitbit and Pixel Watch portfolio.
